Applied AI

An AI agent team that builds the dashboard

I set up a team of 5 agents that works from a Kanban board, talks to me on Slack and ships code through pull requests, only when tests pass.

  • The coordinator, Registro, splits requests into cards of up to 1 hour and assigns them.
  • Review is separate from merge, and related PRs merge as a group to avoid conflicts.
  • Scheduled jobs: morning summary, product questions and a weekly security scan.
  • AI model chosen per role to keep costs down.
  • Every agent mistake became a written rule the whole team follows.

Audit automation · Grant Thornton

From 180 PDF pages to a dashboard in 20 minutes

Every month, 3 PDFs of about 60 pages each arrived with the data for an outsourced-services contract. Extracting it by hand took 2 days.

  • Built a Python script that extracts the data automatically: 2 days became 20 minutes.
  • Sole owner of the Power BI dashboard used in the monthly stakeholder meeting: contract overruns in value or hours, and hours worked above the permitted limit.
  • Audited 30+ expense receipts and service contracts every month.
  • At a construction company, found in the payment reports that site crews' overtime was paid at a 100% premium when the correct rate was 50%. Even the finance manager believed 100% was right; the premium was corrected.

How I work

Rules where it must be exact

Fixed rules for what has to be right every time, like what counts as a sale or how a payment matches an invoice. AI only where judgment helps.

Two checks on every AI output

First the format: required fields present and well formed. Then the business rules: the numbers have to make sense. Anything that fails goes to a person.

The system prepares, people approve

Automations draft, match and flag. A person reviews and decides, and the history stays recorded.

Measure the whole job

I start with a small proof of concept on real cases and measure accuracy and the review work left for the team, not just the model's hit rate.
